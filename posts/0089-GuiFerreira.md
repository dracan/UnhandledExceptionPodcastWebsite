In this episode, I was joined again by [Gui Ferreira](https://guiferreira.me/) - who was last on the show back in [episode 78](/posts/0078-guiferreira/) talking about goals and time management. This time we're talking AI, and specifically a deep dive into Claude Code, which Gui has two [Dometrain courses](https://dometrain.com/author/gui-ferreira/) on! We did a lightning round of our favourite Claude Code features and tips - including `/goal`, dynamic workflows vs `/batch`, auto mode (and the surprise behaviour change that caught me out!), `Ctrl+G` and `Ctrl+S`, voice mode, `/btw`, rewinding, path-scoped rules, and `/simplify`. We recorded two episodes back-to-back, so keep an eye out for the next one, where we chat about using coding agents for things that aren't code!

# Gui Ferreira

Guilherme Ferreira, also known as Gui, is a Minimalist Software Craftsman, passionate about simplicity and continuous learning. After around twenty years as a .NET developer, and a journey through roles including Software Engineer, Architect, CTO, and Developer Advocate, Gui now focuses on education - building his own projects, working with companies, delivering in-person workshops, and creating courses on Dometrain. He's a Microsoft MVP for Developer Technologies, a tech speaker, a YouTuber, and a .NET content creator, and is based near Porto in Portugal.
